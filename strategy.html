The Watering page's villages, with a player: someone playing the game strategically for a goal. Each day before work the player may kill any villager, appoint any grown-ups as scholars (as many as they like) or dismiss them, refill the well to the brim, give any number of 刀 叉 衣 笠 up to SR, for free, and say for each couple whether they may have a child; on the 1st of each month they may give each villager their own order of the day's jobs, and give the village any amount of rice. Pick a strategy to see its villages, in Macro or Micro mode, and how all four compare with the same villages left alone.
