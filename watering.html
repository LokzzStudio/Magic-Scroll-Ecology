Watering

The Clothing page, with water in the fields. Each of the 4 fields has its own water level, from 0 (dry) to 10 (flooded). Once a field has been tended this year it grows rice by itself, by its water: best at levels 3–4, nothing at 1 or 6–7, and rice is lost when it's far too dry or too wet. Tending uses water up, so when a farmer finds their field at level 3 or lower they fetch water: from the well (井) if it has some and is nearer, otherwise from the river (川), which never runs dry. The well refills from the ground, by the land's water level (the Weather page's), and from the rain. A field nobody tends goes back to the land's level within the day.
