Craft

The Resources page, plus crafts at the house. After the jobs above crafts in the Priority order, women weave (紡織) wool and timber into 衣 clothes and 笠 hats, and men forge (鍛造) stone and timber into 刀 blades and 叉 hayforks. Each craft has a tier, from its crafters and its materials. Crafts are used until they wear out: everyone wears 衣 and 笠, and collectors carry 刀 and farmers 叉, which bring in more for the same energy. A collector's 刀 also counts toward the tier of the wool, timber and stone they bring in (no 刀: N).

What each craft takes; this page only

At the house, so there's no walking: one loom (紡織, women) and one forge (鍛造, men), one crafter at each a day. Who may is set with Jobs.

Lasts: the energy of use before it's gone (at the end of that day). 衣 and 笠: all the energy their wearer spends; 刀: collecting; 叉: farming.

To start with

In store on day 1, as if just made, and worn out like made ones; every page after this one starts with them too. An R 刀 or 叉 adds the R bonus below; an R 衣 or 笠 keeps off the cold or the rain on the Clothing page.

A 刀 or 叉 adds its bonus to its holder's effectiveness. A finished craft's tier is round((score + roll) ÷ 2): halfway between its score (the average of its materials' tier, its crafters' skill tier and their human tier) and a lucky roll from 1.01 up to one more than the score. The better the inputs, the harder the roll pulls the tier down; UR takes SSR on all three, and even then it's 1 in 400. See How a craft's tier is drawn.
