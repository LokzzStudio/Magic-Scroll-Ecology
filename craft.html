Craft

The Resources page, with crafts. Once every food job is filled, idle women weave (紡織) wool into 衣 clothes or 笠 hats, and idle men forge (鍛造) stone into 刀 blades or 叉 hayforks, at the house. A craft takes its materials up front and is worked until it's done; there's one loom and one forge, so one weaver and one smith at a time. Nothing uses the crafts yet, so they only pile up; food and school work just as on the Education page.
