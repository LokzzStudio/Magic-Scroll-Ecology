Craft

The Resources page, plus crafts at the house. Once the jobs above crafts in the Priority order are filled, women weave (紡織) wool and timber into 衣 clothes and 笠 hats, and men forge (鍛造) stone and timber into 刀 blades and 叉 hayforks. Each craft has a tier, from its crafters and its materials. Crafts are used until they wear out: everyone wears 衣 and 笠, and collectors carry 刀 and farmers 叉, which make their work go further.

At the house, so there's no walking: one loom (紡織, women) and one forge (鍛造, men), one person at each. Who may is set with Jobs.
