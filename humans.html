Humans

Every person is tracked on their own, as a man or a woman. Couples have children, and food limits how many people can live. Each day runs these five steps in order:

  1. 1 · FoodF is shared by need: c for an adult, 70% of it for a child, 80% for an elder. Anyone short loses k × food missed days of life.
  2. 2 · AgeingAnyone who reaches their lifespan dies.
  3. 3 · Couplesmin(adult men, adult women); elders don't have children
  4. 4 · BirthsMothers who conceived G days ago give birth. Then each couple whose woman isn't expecting, and is young enough to give birth before she's an elder, conceives with chance p. A boy with chance (♀ + X) ÷ (♂ + ♀ + 2X), so births lean to the scarcer sex.
  5. 5 · Life stagesChildren grow up at 360 days and become elders at 3,240.
