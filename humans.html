Humans

Every person is tracked on their own, as a man or a woman. Couples have children, and food limits how many people can live. Each day runs these five steps in order:

  1. 1 · FoodF is split equally. Anyone short of their need c loses K × shortfalln days of life.
  2. 2 · AgeingAnyone who reaches their lifespan dies.
  3. 3 · Couplesmin(adult men, adult women)
  4. 4 · BirthsEach couple: chance p of a child. Boy or girl, 50/50.
  5. 5 · AdultsChildren grow up at 360 days.
