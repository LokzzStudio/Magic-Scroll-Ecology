Attribute

The Production Model, with two attributes on every person: Power (力量) and Intelligence (智力). Work runs on energy: Intelligence sets how fast someone spends it, Power sets how much they have for the day, and each energy collects a set amount of milk (milk effectiveness, from the Food page). A cow gives at most 10 milk a day, so a strong villager milks another cow or puts the energy they have left into the rice fields, where Power and Intelligence pay off. Elders have less of both (the elder penalty). At 100 and 100, everyone under elder age does exactly what they did on the Production Model.

Attributes

Every person has both; this page only

Work durability (耐力): the energy someone can spend in a day. 100 Power = 10 energy; when it's used up, they stop for the day.

Work efficiency (效率): how fast someone spends energy at work. 100 Intelligence = 1 energy an hour. Nobody works more than 10 hours a day.
