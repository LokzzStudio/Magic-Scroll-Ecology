Attribute

The Production Model, with two attributes on every person: Power (力量) and Intelligence (智力). Work runs on energy: Intelligence sets how fast someone spends it, Power sets how much they have for the day, and each milk takes a set amount of energy to collect (work effectiveness, from the Food page). A cow gives as much milk as its milker collects. At 100 and 100 everyone does exactly what they did on the Production Model, so the results match run for run.

Attributes

Every person has both; this page only

Work durability (耐力): the energy someone can spend in a day. 100 Power = 100 energy; when it's used up, they stop for the day.

Work efficiency (效率): how fast someone spends energy at work. 100 Intelligence = 10 energy an hour. Nobody works more than 10 hours a day.

Milk effectiveness10 energy = 1 milk

The same for everyone; set on the Food page. Rice works the same way: its effectiveness (tending energy per rice) is set there too, and harvesting brings in 250 rice per 100 energy.

Work is counted in energy; 100 energy is what a person with 100 Power spends in a day. Milk comes first: every adult cow gets a milker, elders before adults since elders can't farm, and a cow gives as much as its milker collects. Adults left over go to the rice, up to 400 energy of work. Tending (days 1–180): each 100 energy gives 25% of the full crop, so 400 give 100%, and the crop is the average crew. Harvest (days 181–270): each 100 energy brings in 250 rice until the field is bare; rice still standing after day 270 is lost. Days 271–360 have no rice work.

Rationing

From the Advisor Model

Each day the advisor sets a ration: the food in store plus what today's workers will still bring in, spread evenly over the days until the next harvest. Off means everyone eats what they need until it runs out.
