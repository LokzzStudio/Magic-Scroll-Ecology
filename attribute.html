Attribute

The Food page's farm, but now food only comes in if people work for it, on the Map page's map and day. Every person has two attributes: Power (力量) and Intelligence (智力). Work runs on energy: Intelligence sets how fast someone spends it, Power sets how much they have for the day, and each energy collects a set amount of milk (milk effectiveness, from the Food page). Each adult cow takes one milker for the day and each of the 4 rice fields one farmer, so a stronger villager simply gets more out of their cow or their field, and once every cow and field has someone, the rest have no food work. Elders have less of both (the elder penalty). At 100 and 100, everyone under elder age does a standard day: 100 energy at 10 an hour.

Attributes

Every person has both; this page only

Work durability (耐力): the energy someone can spend in a day. 100 Power = 100 energy; when it's used up, they stop for the day.

Work efficiency (效率): how fast someone spends energy at work. 100 Intelligence = 10 energy an hour, for as long as the day out allows (the Map page: about 11 hours once meals are eaten, less the walking).

Milk effectiveness1 energy = 1 milk

The same for everyone; set on the Food page. So is the most a field gives (a cow has no limit: one milker to a cow a day, and it gives as much as they milk). Rice works the same way: its effectiveness (rice per energy of tending) is set there too, and harvesting brings in 25 rice per energy.
