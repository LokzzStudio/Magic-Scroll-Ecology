Attribute

The Food page's farm, but now food only comes in if people work for it, on the Map page's map and day. Every person has two attributes: Power (力量) and Intelligence (智力). Work runs on Power: each morning a villager has their full Power to spend, Intelligence sets how fast they spend it, and each Power spent collects a set amount of milk (milk effectiveness, from the Food page). Each adult cow takes one milker for the day and each of the 4 rice fields one farmer, so a stronger villager simply gets more out of their cow or their field, and once every cow and field has someone, the rest have no food work. Elders have less of both (the elder penalty), and eat that much less. At 100 and 100, everyone under elder age does a standard day: 100 Power at 10 an hour. In short, a villager's Power is both what they can spend in a day and what they eat: they spend it through the day, down to nothing at most, and have it all back the next morning. Short of food, they still have their full Power the next day, but lose a day of life for each food missed (the Humans page's hunger toll).

Every person has both; this page only

Work durability (耐力): how much someone can spend in a day, all of their Power, back in full each morning; when it's used up, they stop for the day.

Work efficiency (效率): how fast someone spends Power at work. 100 Intelligence = 10 Power an hour, for as long as the day out allows (the Map page: about 11 hours once meals are eaten, less the walking).

The same for everyone; set on the Food page. So is the most a field gives (a cow has no limit: one milker to a cow a day, and it gives as much as they milk). Rice works the same way: its effectiveness (rice per Power of tending) is set there too, and harvesting brings in 25 rice per Power.
