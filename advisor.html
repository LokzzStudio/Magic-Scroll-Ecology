Advisor

The Food Model's farm, with a food advisor who decides how much is eaten each day. Without one, everyone eats what they need until the food runs out; with one, the village rations its stores to last until the next harvest.

Food advisor

Each day the advisor sets a ration: the food in store plus what's still to come, spread evenly over the days until the next harvest. Off means everyone eats what they need until it runs out.
