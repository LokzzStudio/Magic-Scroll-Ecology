Map

Where the village's places are, and the shape of everyone's day. People wake between 4 and 8 o'clock and are out of the house for 12 hours. They eat breakfast by the house, walk out to work, and, from 5½ hours after waking, come home for lunch once the Power they're on is done. Whatever they collect they carry home (搬運) after every 10 Power of it, then walk back out. Nobody starts work they couldn't finish and be home from by the end of the 12 hours. Walking eats into the working day, and a meal keeps them full for only so long, so a late walk home is a hungry one.

Map

Used by Attribute and every page after it

Pick a place, then click or drag on the map. Distance is counted in steps (no diagonals) from the nearest house to the nearest tile of each kind, so extra tiles further away don't matter. Going from one place straight to another counts the steps between their nearest tiles. The forest (timber) and the mine (stone) are worked on the Resources page. The well (井) and the river (川) have no use yet.

h

From this hour, anyone idle goes straight home; anyone working goes home as soon as the Power they're on is done (and carried home). They eat lunch by the house and go back out. A full breakfast keeps someone full for half the day out, 6 hours, so lunch after that is a hungry one.

min

Breakfast and lunch are eaten by the house. Each is half the day's ration.

Power

After this much collecting (milking, harvest, timber, wool, stone), a worker walks home with it and back out; whatever's left at the end of the job goes home too. Tending, crafts and class carry nothing.

o'clock

Everyone wakes between these two hours at random, spread evenly. The day out is the same length for everyone, whenever they wake.
