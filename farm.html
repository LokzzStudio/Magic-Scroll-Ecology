Food

Cows and people on one farm for 18,000 days (fifty years of 360 days). People eat two foods: milk from adult cows every day, and rice they harvest in early autumn. A food advisor decides how much is eaten each day, rationing the stores to last until the next harvest; without one, everyone eats what they need until the food runs out.
