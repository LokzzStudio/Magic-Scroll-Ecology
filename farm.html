Full Simulation

Cows and people on one farm for 3600 days (ten years of 360 days). The cows follow the Herd tuner and the people follow the Humans page, including how many of each you start with; only rice farming and the food advisor are set here. People eat two foods: milk from adult cows every day, and rice they harvest in early autumn.
