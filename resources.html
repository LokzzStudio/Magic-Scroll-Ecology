Resources

The Happiness page, plus a flock of sheep on the pasture, a forest, a mine, and skills. After the food work, grown-ups with energy to spare go out to collect: women shear wool (採羊毛) and men quarry stone (採石), or cut wood (伐木) when there's less timber. One person to a place for the day: a shearer to each sheep, and a few quarriers and woodcutters (2 and 3). Nothing uses them yet, so they pile up. Each comes in lots of 100 with a tier (N, R, SR or SSR), from the collector's skill and the tier of the sheep, the forest or the mine it came from. From the Craft page on, the collector's 刀 blade counts too: a lot's tier comes from the average of all three, and a collector without a 刀 counts as N. And everyone has a level from 0 to 4 at each of nine skills (milking, farming, woodcutting, weaving, forging, shearing, quarrying, teaching and poetry): every energy spent on a job is 1 exp in its skill, and each level brings in more for the same energy (+5%, +10%, +20%, +40% by default).

Resources

Sheep, forest and mine; this page only

Sheep follow the cows' herd rules exactly (same settings, their own luck) and live on the pasture. Collecting comes after the food work in the default Priority, so it's the grown-ups without a cow or a field, or with energy left after theirs, who collect, and resources don't cost food. Each of them picks between their own (women wool, men stone) and timber, whichever there's less of; anyone whose pick isn't possible (no sheep or place left for them, or barred in the Jobs table) turns to the next.

1 energy spent on a job = 1 exp in its skill. At a level, every energy spent at that job brings in its bonus more: more milk, more rice from the same field, more wool from the same sheep, more timber, stone, and knowledge from teaching and poetry. At the harvest the standing rice is all there is, so there it's done faster instead. Crafts get done faster. The level is also the person's tier at that skill, for what they collect and craft.
