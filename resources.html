Resources

The Happiness page, plus a flock of sheep on the pasture, a forest, a mine, and skills. Once every food job is filled, grown-ups with energy to spare go out to collect: women shear wool (採羊毛) and men quarry stone (採石), or cut wood (伐木) when there's less timber. Nothing uses them yet, so they pile up. Each comes in lots of 100 with a tier (基本, 中階, 進階 or 稀有), from the collector's skill and the tier of the sheep, the forest or the mine it came from. And everyone has a level from 1 to 5 at each of nine skills (milking, farming, woodcutting, weaving, forging, shearing, quarrying, teaching and poetry): every energy spent on a job is 1 exp in its skill, and each level above 1 makes each energy there 5% more effective.

Resources

Sheep, forest and mine; this page only

Sheep follow the cows' herd rules exactly (same settings, their own luck) and live on the pasture. Only energy left after every food job is filled goes to resources, so resources never cost food. Each idle grown-up picks between their own (women wool, men stone) and timber, whichever there's less of; anyone whose pick isn't possible (no wool left on the sheep, or barred in the Jobs table) turns to the next.

exp

Going from level L to L + 1 takes L × this much more exp. 1 energy spent on a job = 1 exp in its skill.

%

Each level above 1 makes every energy spent at that job do this much more work: more milk, rice, timber, wool or stone, crafts done sooner, more knowledge from teaching and poetry.
