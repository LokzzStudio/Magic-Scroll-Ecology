Resources

Sheep, forest and mine; this page only

Sheep follow the cows' herd rules exactly (same settings, their own luck) and live on the pasture. Only energy left after every food job is filled goes to resources, so resources never cost food. While timber is under its target, the first idle grown-up who may cut wood goes to the forest. The others: women shear first and men quarry first; anyone whose first job isn't possible (no wool left on the sheep, or barred in the Jobs table) turns to the other.
