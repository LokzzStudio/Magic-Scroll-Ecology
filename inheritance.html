Inheritance

The Attribute page, with families: children take after their parents. Every newborn starts with the same Power (力量), 70; their Intelligence (智力) comes from an adult man and woman drawn at random: a share of their average, give or take a random variation. Coming of age adds 30 to both. Milk goes to the children first: milk they drink above their need adds Power as they grow, for good, while going hungry as a child takes Power away, even below what they were born with. Rice doesn't build Power, so milking for the children is time not spent in the fields. Without that extra milk, a village drifts toward the same level whatever it starts at.
