Inheritance

The Attribute page, with families: children take after their parents. A newborn's Power (力量) and Intelligence (智力) come from an adult man and woman drawn at random: a share of their average, give or take a random variation. Coming of age adds 30 to both, and food that would otherwise spoil, fed to the children, adds Power for life. Without that extra food, a village drifts toward the same level whatever it starts at.
