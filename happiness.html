Happiness

The Education page, with happiness. Everyone has a happiness from 0 to 100: the cleverer they are, the less content (150 − ⌊Intelligence ÷ 1⌋, at least 0), and the Power they have left over at the end of a day makes them happier that night and through the next day, by 1 for each Power; 100 at most in all. Couples now pair for life (adults without a partner pair up at random), and a happy couple is more likely to have a child, while both are adults: happiness 50 is the Humans page's chance, 0 is none, 100 is double.
