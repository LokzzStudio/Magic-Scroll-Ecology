Happiness

The Education page, with happiness. Everyone has a happiness from 0 to 100: the cleverer they are, the less content (100 − ⌊Intelligence ÷ 2⌋), and the energy they have left over at the end of a day makes them happier that night and through the next day, by its square root, rounded down. Couples now pair for life (adults without a partner pair up at random), and a happy couple is more likely to have a child, while both are adults: happiness 50 is the Humans page's chance, 0 is none, 100 is double.

What makes people happy; this page only

Int.

Base happiness = 100 − ⌊Intelligence ÷ this⌋, at least 0.

Extra happiness = ⌊√(energy left)⌋: energy left unspent at the end of a day adds to happiness from that night (the night's births go by it) through the next day, up to 100. 4 energy left is +2, 25 is +5, 100 is +10: the first few energy count the most.
