Education

The Inheritance page, with a school. The village appoints its adult with the highest Intelligence (智力) as its Scholar (文人), if it can spare them from the food work, and a scholar does no food work until they become an elder: they teach the children, and spend any energy the lessons leave on poetry (吟詩). A class shares its lesson out: a scholar with two children gives each half as much. More scholars are possible (Most scholars): then the children are split evenly among them. A child takes home knowledge by how much energy they keep up with, and it buys Intelligence for life, dearer the brighter they already are.

Education

Scholars teach the children; this page only

scholars

Each morning, while there are fewer scholars than this and than the children, the village appoints its adult with the highest Intelligence as Scholar (文人), if it can spare them: without the scholars and the children, there must still be a full crew of 4 grown-ups who may tend the fields, and from a second scholar on, enough milkers left to milk the village's need. Once appointed, a scholar stays, even if food work later runs short or the children grow up, until they become an elder (then they're like any other elder) or die. Scholars do no other work: any energy the lessons leave, they spend on poetry. In the job, they work with more Intelligence and less Power (the scholar adjustment). Every child comes to class; with more than one scholar, each day the children are split among them as evenly as can be, at random.

1 energy =knowledge

The knowledge each energy a scholar spends teaching gives. A child learns while both are in class (each wakes and goes home for lunch on their own clock), until either runs out of energy. The child takes home the smaller of the two's energy spent over that time, as knowledge at this rate, divided by the children in the class.
