Education

One scholar teaches the children; this page only

The village picks its adult with the highest Intelligence as Scholar (文人). The village only appoints one when it can spare them: the grown-ups left must still fill every cow and all the fields, without the children. Once appointed, the scholar stays, even if food work later runs short, until they become an elder (then they're like any other elder) or die. The scholar does no other work: any energy the lessons leave, they spend on poetry. In the job, they work with more Intelligence and less Power (the scholar adjustment). Every child comes to class. The scholar is also the food advisor; with no scholar, nobody rations (see Rationing).

1 energy =knowledge

The knowledge each energy the scholar spends teaching or on poetry gives. A child's lesson lasts until the earlier of them or the scholar runs out of energy; the child takes home the share of it their own energy keeps up with (child's energy ÷ scholar's energy, at most all of it).
