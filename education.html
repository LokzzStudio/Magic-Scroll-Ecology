Education

The Inheritance page, with a school. The village appoints its adults with the highest Intelligence (智力) as Scholars (文人), one for each child, but only while it can spare them from the food work, and a scholar does no food work until they become an elder: they teach the children, and spend any energy the lessons leave on poetry (吟詩). The children are split evenly among the scholars, and a class shares its lesson out: a scholar with two children gives each half as much. A child takes home knowledge by how much energy they keep up with, and it buys Intelligence for life, dearer the brighter they already are. The brightest scholar is also the village's food advisor, with their Intelligence at work as the advisor's IQ; with no scholar, nobody rations.

Education

Scholars teach the children; this page only

Each morning, while there are fewer scholars than children (or none at all), the village appoints its adult with the highest Intelligence as Scholar (文人), if it can spare them: without the scholars and the children, there must still be a full crew of 4 grown-ups who may tend the fields. Once appointed, a scholar stays, even if food work later runs short or the children grow up, until they become an elder (then they're like any other elder) or die. Scholars do no other work: any energy the lessons leave, they spend on poetry. In the job, they work with more Intelligence and less Power (the scholar adjustment). Every child comes to class, to a scholar drawn each day: the children are split as evenly as can be. The brightest scholar is also the food advisor; with no scholar, nobody rations (see Rationing).

1 energy =knowledge

The knowledge each energy a scholar spends teaching gives. A child learns while both are in class (each wakes and goes home for lunch on their own clock), until either runs out of energy. The child takes home the smaller of the two's energy spent over that time, as knowledge at this rate, divided by the children in the class.
